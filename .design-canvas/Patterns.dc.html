Interior pages open with 12vh of air above the heading and 10–12vh below it. Breakpoint is a single 620px — grids collapse to one column, hover-only affordances disappear.

Motion
--easecubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) the default — most transitions
--ease-snapcubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.1, 1) snappier — KABOOM moments

Durations sit between 0.2s and 0.5s: 0.2–0.25s for color, 0.3–0.35s for borders and opacity, 0.45s for the title slide. Menu links stagger in at 55ms per item.

Per-page accent — --page

Each route sets --page on its wrapper. Section labels, kickers, back-links and case-study links inherit it, so a page reads as one color without any of them hard-coding a hex. Crimson stays reserved for interaction; these are identity.

Accessibility rules
  • Focus ring is crimson, 2px, 3px offset — on every focusable. #main is exempt (focused programmatically on route change).
  • Every hover state is mirrored with :focus-within or :focus-visible so keyboard reaches it.
  • Below 620px, hover-dependent content is shown unconditionally (work blurbs) or hidden (trailing preview, callouts, hero hint).
  • --dim and --muted both pass AA on --void. Disabled links use --muted, never opacity — opacity would fail contrast.
  • Small links carry ±10px padding/margin for a larger tap target.
  • prefers-reduced-motion kills all transitions and animations globally; components also guard in JS.
  • A skip link sits off-screen until focused.
Surface rules
No radii

Everything is square except the 50% dots and the hero's 2px placeholder frame. No rounded cards.

No shadows

The one exception is the work-row dot's 0 0 14px glow on hover — light, not depth.

Hairlines only

Structure comes from 1px --line rules and whitespace — never fills or boxes.
